## World Window videos

World Window bundles no video files and requires no API key. Zen ships a curated metadata catalog of more than forty free nature scenes selected from Mixkit's nature collection, then streams Mixkit's standard 720p MP4 delivery files from `assets.mixkit.co`. Scenes remain subject to the [Mixkit Free License](https://mixkit.co/license/#videoFree). Every included CDN URL was checked for `HTTP 200` and `video/mp4`; the catalog deliberately excludes people-focused, vehicle-focused, wedding, and aggressive aerial footage.
